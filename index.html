Chai Break Studios Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy covers the Android games made by Chai Break Studios that link to this page. The short version: our games do not collect personal information, have no ads and no analytics, and work offline. The one exception is the optional online match in Book Cricket, described under Online play.

Information we collect

None. The games do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers or any other personal information. They do not send anything about you over the internet, except the limited match data used by Book Cricket's optional online play (see below).

Information stored on your phone

Each game saves its own progress and settings (for example levels finished, coins, sound and vibration choices) on your device so it can remember them next time. This stays on your phone, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Online play (Book Cricket only)

If you choose Online Match in Book Cricket, the game signs you in as an anonymous guest using Google Firebase. You don't enter a name, email or password. To run the match it sends to Google Firebase servers (Singapore region): a random guest ID created by Firebase, your team label (a class and section plus a nickname picked from a fixed list, never text you type), which book you play with, and your match moves (how many pages you have flipped, and a random number both phones use to make the flips fair). Your opponent sees your team label and score. There is no chat.

This data is used only to run the match. It is not sold or shared, and it is not used for ads or analytics. A finished match is deleted from the server; a waiting-list entry is removed when you stop searching; an abandoned match may stay on the server until we clear it. Guest IDs are deleted automatically after 30 days. If you never open Online Match, nothing is sent. Google's handling of Firebase data is described in Firebase's privacy information.

Purchases

Some games offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play of which item you bought, so it can unlock it. Google's handling of purchases is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads and analytics

None of the games below show ads or use analytics or tracking tools, and none use the advertising ID.

Permissions

Some games use vibration for short haptic feedback, which you can switch off in the game's settings. Some games let you share a picture or score using Android's share menu; nothing is shared unless you choose to. The games do not use the camera, microphone or location.

Children

The games do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Book Cricket's optional online play uses only an anonymous guest ID and a team label from a fixed list.

Games covered

Leak Patrol

Pipe puzzle. Saves progress on your phone; can share a Daily pipe result (text only) through Android's share menu if you choose, and has no in-app purchases.

Pencil Bridge

Draw-a-bridge physics game. Saves levels and stars on your phone and has no in-app purchases.

Doodle Zoo

Draw-your-own-animal zoo. Saves your zoo on your phone. Optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

Desi Jigsaw Puzzle

Jigsaw puzzles. Saves progress on your phone; can share a finished picture through Android's share menu if you choose. Optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

Book Cricket

Notebook cricket game. Saves matches and settings on your phone. Optional online matches against random players send the limited data described under Online play. This version has no in-app purchases.

Pen Fight

Desk pen-flicking game. Saves progress on your phone and has no in-app purchases.

Chappal Attack

Arcade dodging game. Saves scores on your phone; this version has no in-app purchases.

Bone Breaker: Ragdoll Fall

Cartoon ragdoll physics game. Saves progress on your phone; can share a clip or score through Android's share menu if you choose. Optional in-app purchases through Google Play.
