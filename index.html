Chai Break Studios Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Android games made by Chai Break Studios that link to this page. The short version: our games do not collect personal information, have no ads and no analytics, and work offline.

Information we collect

None. The games do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers or any other personal information. They do not send anything about you over the internet.

Information stored on your phone

Each game saves its own progress and settings (for example levels finished, coins, sound and vibration choices) on your device so it can remember them next time. This stays on your phone, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Purchases

Some games offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google Play of which item you bought, so it can unlock it. Google's handling of purchases is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Ads and analytics

None of the games below show ads or use analytics or tracking tools, and none use the advertising ID.

Permissions

Some games use vibration for short haptic feedback, which you can switch off in the game's settings. Some games let you share a picture or score using Android's share menu; nothing is shared unless you choose to. The games do not use the camera, microphone or location.

Children

The games do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
